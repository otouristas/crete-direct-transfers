## Likely competitive strengths to preserve [High]

- Clear fixed-price route records with distance, duration and vehicle pricing.
- Local operational detail for HER, CHQ, Souda and Crete resorts.
- Named operations authors and practical arrival guides.
- Flight/ferry tracking, waiting-time and meet-and-greet explanations.
- Multi-language architecture and structured data.

## Gaps to test against actual SERPs

1. **Proof and trust** [High]: licensing evidence, partner-vetting process, insurance, real contact details, review provenance and independently verifiable business identity.
2. **Policy consistency** [High]: child-seat guidance was corrected and cited; prices, waiting terms and operating claims still require owner verification.
3. **Route comprehensiveness** [Medium]: exact pickup/drop-off maps, return-flight timing, seasonal road notes and hotel access details.
4. **Transparent comparisons** [Medium]: assumptions and timestamps for taxi, bus and private-transfer price comparisons.
5. **Original media** [Medium]: route/meeting-point photographs and maps rather than generic imagery.
6. **Market depth outside Crete** [High]: quote-market airport/city pages risk being thinner than established local operators.
7. **Editorial evidence** [Medium]: cited official sources, update logs and author profile pages.
8. **SERP-format coverage** [Low]: sampled operators repeatedly foreground fixed-price and route-specific booking language; concise answer blocks, comparisons, FAQs and route facts remain useful differentiators. See [Airports Taxi Transfers](https://airportstaxitransfers.com/transportation/taxi/crete-transfers), [CreteCab](https://www.crete.cab/de/) and [Aéroport Héraklion](https://aeroportheraklion.fr/taxi/).
