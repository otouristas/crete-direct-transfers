# Multilingual Crete transfer SERP sample

Method: Firecrawl web search on 2026-08-23. This is a point-in-time, non-personalized research sample, not a stable ranking report. Search volume, clicks, impressions, conversions and keyword difficulty are **NOT AVAILABLE**.

## Cross-locale finding

Fixed-price wording, airport/city specificity, private-service language and practical booking facts recur across the sampled results. English results explicitly surface “fixed pricing” and vehicle-level fare framing in [Airports Taxi Transfers](https://airportstaxitransfers.com/transportation/taxi/crete-transfers), [Kreta Rides](https://www.kretarides.com/prices) and [Suntransfers](https://www.suntransfers.com/crete-heraklion-airport). The same intent appears as “Festpreis” in [CreteCab](https://www.crete.cab/de/), “prix fixe” in [Aéroport Héraklion](https://aeroportheraklion.fr/taxi/), “prezzo fisso” in [Transfeero Italia](https://www.transfeero.com/it/trasferimenti-dallaeroporto-di-creta-heraklion-her/), “vaste prijs” in [Taxi2Airport Nederland](https://www.taxi2airport.com/nl/destinations/iraklio-greece/heraklion-airport), and fixed/private airport wording in [Transfeero España](https://www.transfeero.com/es/traslados-aeropuerto-de-creta-heraklion-her/traslado-desde-el-aeropuerto-de-heraklion-al-centro-de-la-ciudad-de-heraklion/).

## Content implications

- Preserve native commercial terms rather than translating English word order mechanically.
- Lead airport-route pages with origin, destination, private service, fixed/confirmed total, meeting process and booking mode.
- Do not copy competitor prices, review claims or cancellation promises; those vary by operator and date.
- Use route pages for exact journey intent and editorial pages for comparison/logistics intent.
