# TransferAround brand voice

Source basis: repository route, airport, about, legal and editorial copy reviewed 2026-08-23. Confidence: **CONFIRMED for tone**, operational claims still require owner verification.

## Voice

- Direct: answer route, meeting-point, timing and booking questions before selling.
- Local: use specific airport, port, road and pedestrian-access context only when it is verified.
- Transparent: separate instant Crete booking from partner-confirmed quote coverage.
- Calm: explain caveats without fear, hype or manufactured urgency.
- Human: short sentences, concrete nouns and practical choices; no generic travel inspiration.

## Preferred language

Use “fixed total confirmed before payment,” “per vehicle,” “meeting point,” “flight number,” “licensed local operator,” “route-specific quote,” and “nearest vehicle-accessible point.”

Avoid “best,” “cheapest,” “guaranteed,” “seamless,” “world-class,” “unbeatable,” “availability is running out,” and claims about reviews, prices, support hours or driver history unless the current page can prove them.

## Page pattern

1. Give the direct answer.
2. State what the price or quote includes.
3. Explain the local constraint or caveat.
4. Show the relevant evidence, policy or source.
5. Offer one route-specific next step.

## Marketplace truth

Crete is the operational base with instant-bookable routes. Journeys elsewhere are quote mode: availability, operator, vehicle, meeting point and total are confirmed before payment. Copy and structured data must not present a generated landing page as proof that a driver or a public “from” price already exists.
