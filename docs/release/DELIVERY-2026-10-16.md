## Product changes

Native onboarding, GPS pickup, road polylines/distance/approximate time, eight aligned vehicle classes, passenger/luggage/flight details, demo payment in normal booking, timed Driver acceptance, ordered journey stages and protected live GPS. The web dashboards read those same test and paid data sources separately. The approved icon is used without nested glass framing on the native map.

Ride with AI is available in Traveler and on the website. It asks for missing details, resolves an exact place selection, proposes a capacity-matched vehicle and hands the itinerary into booking for review. Date/time uses the pickup's local time zone. Choosing an address does not spend another model call. The AI cannot confirm a payment, book automatically, assign a driver or claim verified flight status. GPS-button coordinates are excluded from the model payload and message submission requires explicit consent.
